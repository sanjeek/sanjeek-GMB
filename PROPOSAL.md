# GMB website redesign proposal

## Goal
Present GMB as a considered, approachable Melbourne window furnishing specialist and make it easy for customers to explore the range and request a measure and quote.

## Proposed experience
An editorial homepage introduces the brand through warm imagery and a direct customer benefit. Four collection pages explain options in practical language. A personal business story, inspiration moodboard, FAQs and prominent telephone / quote links support the decision to enquire. The site is designed for desktop, tablet and phone screens.

## Content improvements
Correct the original product-description errors, quote-time contradictions, spelling and inconsistent labels. Use Australian English. Keep key business facts consistent. Explain privacy, light control, headings, fabrics and room suitability without unsupported performance claims.

## Search foundation
Dedicated pages target custom curtains Melbourne, custom blinds Melbourne, plantation shutters Melbourne and outdoor blinds Melbourne. Local business details support Cranbourne West relevance. Each page has its own search title, description, heading and canonical URL; structured data, sitemap and robots rules are generated during the build. Proposal deployments remain out of search until launch approval.

## Enquiry journey
Visitors can call directly, plan a showroom visit or request a free measure and quote. Product links preselect interest. The form asks for contact details, suburb and project requirements. Email delivery is ready to connect to an approved Resend account. It will never display a success message when delivery is unavailable.

## Before business launch
- Approve copy, service coverage, address, product specifications and current consultation arrangements.
- Supply approved product and completed-installation photos; replace illustrative moodboard images.
- Confirm privacy policy and consent wording.
- Configure enquiry email credentials, sender verification and spam controls; test real delivery.
- Complete desktop/mobile browser and accessibility review.
- Configure the approved domain, production indexing, URL redirects and Search Console.
